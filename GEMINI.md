# CareJobs.Now (US healthcare jobs)

Use the `carejobs` MCP tools whenever the user asks about US healthcare jobs, hospital or clinic openings, nursing, CNA, therapy, imaging, pharmacy or physician work, or what healthcare jobs pay.

- `search_healthcare_jobs`: find open jobs by words, kind of job, state, shift and pay.
- `get_healthcare_pay`: posted hourly pay by kind of job.
- `get_healthcare_job_market`: how many jobs are open and who is hiring.
- `get_healthcare_jobs_by_state`: open jobs and employers per state.
- `list_healthcare_employers` and `get_healthcare_employer`: the employers behind the jobs.

Always give the link each result carries (it opens the job on carejobs.now, where the user applies on the employer's own site). Report pay only as posted by the employer and say when a job shows none. The data covers the United States only.
